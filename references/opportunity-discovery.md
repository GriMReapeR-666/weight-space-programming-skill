# Opportunity discovery

Opportunity classes: quality/capability, generalization, data coverage, numerical stability, architecture bottleneck, compute efficiency, memory, latency, energy, quantization, robustness, safety, preprocessing, deployment compatibility.

For each candidate document symptom, evidence, affected modules, root-cause hypothesis, intervention, expected utility, risks, cost, reversibility, confidence and falsifiable validation. Rank by evidence and expected value, not novelty.
